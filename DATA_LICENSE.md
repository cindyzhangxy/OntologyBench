# Data licensing

The benchmark task files in `src/ontologybench/data/tasks_global/` are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cite OntologyBench when redistributing or adapting them.

OntologyBench task artifacts were derived from public biomedical resources, including the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO), Mondo Disease Ontology, HGNC, and NCBI Gene. Those upstream resources retain their own licenses and attribution requirements. Users who reconstruct or redistribute source-derived content are responsible for reviewing the current terms of each upstream provider.
